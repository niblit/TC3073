The Week 3 roadmap requires designing a layered transformation taxonomy encompassing content, structure, and channel-metadata.

- **Content-Level:** This layer focuses on textual changes, explicitly including paraphrase, synonym substitution, and obfuscation.
    
- **Structure-Level:** This involves structure edits. You could approach this by reordering sentences, shuffling paragraph layouts, or injecting benign corporate boilerplate to dilute the density of malicious tokens.
    
- **Channel-Metadata-Level:** This involves metadata edits. You might approach this by spoofing sender display names, manipulating routing headers (like Reply-To), or substituting look-alike domains to test how much weight the detector assigns to the delivery envelope.
